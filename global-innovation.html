Cross-Border Digital Education Platforms Bridge Skills Gap for Millions

GENEVA / WASHINGTON — New collaborative online learning networks are enabling students across continents to access world-class programming, engineering, and digital science curricula completely free of charge.

By partnering with international universities and open-source tech foundations, these platforms have successfully enrolled millions of students from remote and underserved regions, leveling the global professional playing field.

The Future of Remote Workforce Development

Education experts project that borderless digital learning ecosystems will generate the largest remote technical workforce in history over the coming decade.
