Community Leaders Advocate for Enhanced Infrastructure Funding

Photo: Stakeholders meeting during the local development roundtable in Kaduna.

KADUNA — Key community stakeholders and local leaders from across various districts met earlier this week in a high-level consultative forum, strongly advocating for targeted enhancements in grassroots infrastructure funding.

The dialogue focused on accelerating communal development projects, improving inner-city access roads, and ensuring that public utilities reach underserved residential layouts within the metropolis.

"Sustainable community growth depends heavily on active collaboration between local authorities and grassroots representatives," noted one of the presiding community leaders during the session. "We are urging swift action on prioritized developmental milestones."

Local council representatives who attended the forum pledged to review the submitted proposals and integrate community feedback into upcoming fiscal allocations.
