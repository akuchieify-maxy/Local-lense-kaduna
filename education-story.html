State University Launches New Digital Skills Initiative for Undergraduates

Photo: Students participating in a computer and coding training workshop.

KADUNA — In a strategic move to bridge the growing digital literacy gap, the state university has officially launched a comprehensive digital skills development initiative targeted at undergraduates across various faculties.

The program is designed to equip students with practical coding competencies, web technologies, and data handling fundamentals alongside their conventional academic curricula. University authorities note that the initiative aims to prepare graduates for modern job markets and self-employment opportunities in the tech ecosystem.

"The future of work is heavily driven by technology," shared a faculty representative during the opening rollout. "We want our students to graduate not just with theoretical knowledge, but with tangible digital tools they can build solutions with immediately."

Undergraduates have expressed enthusiasm for the program, with many registering for evening practical coding sessions and collaborative workshops scheduled to run through the current semester.
