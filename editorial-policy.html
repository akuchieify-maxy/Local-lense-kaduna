Editorial Policy

Our commitment to accuracy, integrity, and independent local journalism.

At Local Lense Kaduna, we hold ourselves to rigorous journalistic standards. Our primary objective is to inform our readers with fair, accurate, verified, and timely reporting on matters affecting Kaduna State and its communities.

1. Accuracy & Fact-Checking

We make every reasonable effort to verify facts, dates, figures, and statements before publication. Our newsroom cross-references details with credible sources, local authorities, and eyewitness accounts.

2. Editorial Independence

Our editorial decisions are driven entirely by news value and public interest. Sponsored content or commercial advertisements are clearly distinguished from independent editorial reporting so readers always know the nature of the content.

3. Corrections & Updates

Transparency is vital to trust. If an error is identified in any published report, we correct it promptly and transparently. Readers who spot inaccuracies are encouraged to reach out via our contact desk.

4. Fairness & Respect

We treat subjects of our reports, sources, and members of the public with fairness and dignity. We uphold the right of reply whenever significant allegations are reported.

For questions regarding our editorial guidelines or to submit corrections, please contact our editorial desk at ifeanyichukwuma2025@gmail.com.
