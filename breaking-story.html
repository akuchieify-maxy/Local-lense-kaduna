Kaduna State Government Announces New Infrastructural Blueprint for Metropolitan Roads

Photo: Urban development and road networks across Kaduna Metropolis.

KADUNA — In a major policy update aimed at improving urban mobility and driving commercial growth, the Kaduna State Government has unveiled a comprehensive new infrastructural blueprint targeted at metropolitan road networks.

The announcement, made earlier today by state officials, outlines plans for major expansions, rehabilitation of critical inner-city arteries, and improved drainage systems designed to ease traffic congestion during peak business hours.

Transport unions, local business owners, and residents across major commercial districts have expressed optimism regarding the project, noting that improved connectivity will significantly reduce transit times for agricultural produce and general trade goods moving across regional markets.

According to the state's ministry of works, full mobilization of equipment and engineering teams is scheduled to begin across selected zones next week. Local Lense Kaduna will continue to track developments and provide real-time updates as the project rolls out.
